Person Search ​

Concept ​

A search finds people in the SIMAST social registry. You send a list of persons (name, first name, birth date, NINU…); for each one, SIMAST tells you whether it was found and, if so, gives you their household.

Use it when you need to link people you know (a complaint, a field list, a beneficiary roster…) to the households SIMAST delivered to you.

Where SIMAST looks: the scope ​

A search is attached to one of your resources — a project, an activity, a request or a request detail. SIMAST only looks among the households already delivered to you under that resource:

Attached toHouseholds searched
projects/{uuid}Every household delivered for the project's activities
activities/{uuid}Every household delivered for the activity's requests
demandes/{uuid}Every household delivered for the request
demande-details/{uuid}Every household delivered for the request, extensions included

Person status ​

StatusMeaningWhat to do
REVISIONBeing processed, or waiting for a SIMAST analystWait for the next search.updated webhook
MATCHThe person is identifiedRead menage_code, and the household in Search Data
NO_MATCHThe person is not in the registry, confirmed by an analystRead reason (see below)

NO_MATCH is always the result of a human review: an automatic process never concludes on its own that a person is absent. When needed, the analyst searches the whole registry, beyond your scope; a person found this way is delivered as a MATCH like the others.

Reasons ​

reason.valueMeaningWhat to do
ABSENTE_DU_REGISTRENo plausible candidate in the whole registry: the person was not surveyedNothing to retry
DONNEES_INSUFFISANTESToo little information to decideComplete the person's data and send it again in a sub-search
DOUBLON_AMBIGUSeveral plausible candidates that cannot be told apartAdd a distinguishing criterion (birth date, NINU, phone) and send it again

Criteria ​

Each person needs at least a ninu, or nom + prenom, or nom + date_naissance. More criteria give better results: sexe, date_naissance, telephone and commune help tell people with the same name apart.

Up to 1000 persons per search. Give each person an external_ref (your own reference): it is returned with every result and in the webhook.

Delivered data ​

For each person in MATCH, the household is available in Search Data with a fixed set of fields, enough to check the person's identity visually (for example against the ID card the person shows):

  • household: code, version, departement, commune, section_communale, chef_nom, chef_prenom, telephone_1, telephone_2;
  • members: rang, nom, prenom, ninu (CIN / NINU), lien_chef (relationship to the head).

Each person in MATCH carries menage_code, membre_rang and menage_version: the household code, the member rang and the survey version they refer to.

To get the households' data in the format of your request (its fields) and check that they still meet its eligibility criteria, create an inclusion sub-request with include.searches.

Sub-searches ​

To send more persons later (a new batch, corrected data…), create a sub-search under the first search rather than a new search. It inherits the scope, its results are included in the parent's summary, items and data, and a household found by both is delivered once.

Typical flow ​

  1. Create the search → 202, status PENDING.
  2. Receive the search.updated webhook when the automatic processing ends — usually a few seconds. If the processing fails, the webhook tells you so (data.search.status is FAILED) and the persons stay in REVISION.
  3. Read the persons and the households of the MATCH.
  4. Persons in REVISION are reviewed by a SIMAST analyst: you receive a new search.updated after each review session.
  5. To get the data of your request for the households found, create an inclusion sub-request with include.searches, then read Get Data List.
